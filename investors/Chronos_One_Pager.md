# Chronos

## The Decision Layer for AI

### Problem

AI has become incredibly good at generating answers, but it struggles
with long-term work.

Every new conversation starts with incomplete context. Users repeatedly
explain their projects, reconstruct previous decisions, and search
across documents, chats, and notes to recover information that AI has
forgotten.

As projects grow more complex, the bottleneck is no longer
intelligence---it's continuity.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

### Solution

Chronos is a persistent AI workspace built for long-term decision
making.

Instead of treating AI as isolated conversations, Chronos organizes
projects into workspaces where goals, knowledge, decisions, tasks, and
timelines remain connected over time.

Rather than restarting every session, AI can reason from the complete
history of a project.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

### Why Now

Foundation models have reached a point where generating answers is no
longer the primary challenge.

The next generation of AI will require systems that preserve context,
structure reasoning, and maintain continuity across weeks, months, and
years of work.

We believe the missing layer isn't another model---it's decision
infrastructure.
