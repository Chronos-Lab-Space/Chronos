# Chronos --- One Pager

## The Decision Layer for AI

### Problem

Today's AI is excellent at generating answers but poor at supporting
long-term work. Every conversation starts with incomplete context,
forcing users to repeat information, reconstruct previous decisions, and
manage fragmented project knowledge across multiple tools.

As projects become more complex, the limitation isn't
intelligence---it's continuity.

### Solution

Chronos is a persistent AI workspace for long-term decision making.

Instead of treating AI as isolated conversations, Chronos organizes
workspaces where goals, knowledge, decisions, tasks, and project history
remain connected over time.

### Why Now

Foundation models are improving rapidly. The next challenge is
continuity---helping AI reason across weeks, months, and years instead
of isolated conversations.

### Product

**Public Beta** - Persistent workspaces - Goal management - Knowledge
management - Decision engine - Context pipeline - Timeline - Task
management - Authentication

**Roadmap** - LLM integration - Multi-model support - Workspace memory -
Collaboration - Agent orchestration

### Target Users

Founders, developers, researchers, product teams, and knowledge workers.

### Traction

-   Public Beta launched
-   6 early users onboarded
-   Continuous iteration from user feedback

### Business Model

Subscription SaaS with premium AI capabilities, enterprise features, and
future developer APIs.

### Vision

We're building the decision layer for AI---persistent context,
structured reasoning, and long-term collaboration.
